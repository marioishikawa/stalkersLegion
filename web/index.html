Stalkers Legion

Eleven biomes of open ocean, ninety seconds of air, and a survival knife. The kelp forest is full of scrap metal — and the stalkers that chew on it. Seven leviathans are out there: one of them robs you, one of them is the only light on the abyssal plain, one of them heals faster than most divers can cut, and somewhere a long way off there is an island with six things living on it — one of which owns the beach.

Catalogue every species and kill every leviathan. Anything you kill is something you catalogued — all except the Glasswhale, which cannot be killed and has to be scanned like anything else. Finish and the world is yours to keep: carry on diving it, and it can never be deleted.
